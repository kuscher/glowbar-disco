# Data safety

**Does the app collect or share any of the required user data types?** No.

The app has no permissions and no internet access, keeps nothing on the device and sends nothing anywhere. Its only
action is to ask Android to open `glowbar://disco`. What Glowbar Disco itself does (its microphone question for the
music sync) belongs to Google's Glowbar Disco app, not to this one.

The privacy page (googlebook.studio/privacy/glowbar-disco) says the same.
