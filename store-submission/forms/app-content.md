# App content answers

- **Privacy policy:** https://googlebook.studio/privacy/glowbar-disco
- **Ads:** none.
- **App access:** everything is available without sign-in; there is no account.
- **Content rating:** "All other app types"; a utility with no user-generated content, no communication between
  users, no purchases, no location sharing, no web content. Expect Everyone / PEGI 3.
- **Target audience:** 13 and over. Not designed for children.
- **News, COVID-19, government, financial features, health:** none.
- **Data safety:** see data-safety.md (nothing collected, nothing shared).
- **Permissions:** none. No `<queries>`: it starts `ACTION_VIEW glowbar://disco` and Android picks Glowbar.
- **Devices:** `android.hardware.type.pc` is required and minSdk is 37, so Play offers it to the Googlebooks only;
  in the device catalog, exclude "Google Play Games on PC" (search "kiwi").
- **Category:** Tools. **Tags:** launcher, personalization.
- **Contact:** kuscher.projects@gmail.com. **Website:** https://github.com/kuscher/glowbar-disco
- **Impersonation:** the title says "Shortcut", and the descriptions say that Glowbar Disco is Google's and that this
  app is independent. The launcher label is "Glowbar Disco" because that is what the icon opens.
